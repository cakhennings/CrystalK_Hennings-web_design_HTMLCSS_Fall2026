Classic Chicken Pot Pie Recipe

Introduction

There is nothing quite like sitting down to a hot dish of comfort food on a cold day. Having been born in the Fall season, winter is my least favorite season. The days are far too short and the weather way too cold. Winter isn't all bad. Holiday celebrations, family gatherings, and comfort food and drinks make the season bearable. Of all comfort food dishes, chicken pot pie is one of my favorite dishes - when well prepared. I prefer it loaded with lots of different vegetables and a buttery, flaky crust on the top and bottom. I'm not referring to the little frozen number from the days of childhood. I mean a big, hearty, homemade, grown-up, delicious chicken pot pie. While the arrival of Autumn and Winter always leaves me wistful for warm, long, summer days, I have always looked forward to enjoying the myriad comforting dishes that abound during the colder season. The chicken pot pie recipe that follows is one that I particularly enjoy!

Ingredients

Crust
  • 1 box (14.1 oz) refrigerated pie crusts (2 Count), softened as directed on box
Filling
  • 1/3 cup butter or margarine
  • 1/3 cup chopped onion
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 3/4 cups from 1 carton Progresso™ Classic Chicken Broth
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 1/2 cups shredded cooked chicken or turkey
  • 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables, thawed

Recipe Directions

  1. Bring crusts to room temperature by removing outer package and placing on your counter for 15 minutes. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Place 1 pie crust in ungreased 9-inch glass pie plate. Press crust firmly against side and bottom, allowing any extra crust to hang over the edge.
  3. In 2-quart saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Add onion; cook 2 minutes, stirring frequently, until tender.
  4. Stir in flour, salt, and pepper until well blended.
  5. Gradually stir in broth and milk, cooking and stirring until bubbly and thickened.
  6. Stir in chicken and mixed vegetables until thoroughly combined. Remove from heat.
  7. Spoon chicken mixture into crust-lined pie plate.
  8. Place second crust over the chicken mixture. Fold excess top crust under the edge of the bottom crust, pressing edges together to seal and create a thick rim.
  9. Flute the edge of the pie crust by placing your thumb and index finger about 1 inch apart on the outside edge. With your other thumb, push the pastry toward the outside to form a scalloped edge.
  10. With a small knife, cut slits or shapes in several places in top crust to allow steam to escape while baking.
  11. Stir in chicken and mixed vegetables until thoroughly combined. Remove from heat.Bake 15 minutes. Then cover edge of crust with foil to prevent excessive browning.
  12. Bake an additional 15 to 25 minutes or until the top crust is golden brown and filling is at least 165°F. Let stand 5 minutes before serving.
