A Little About Me


I feel a little bit of joy each time I create a computer program that works to solve the problem it is intended to solve. I began my software development journey tinkering at home with web development, learning HTML, CSS, and Javascript independently following youtube videos and various books and CDs on the subject. Later, I decided that I enjoyed it enough that I wanted to get more formal instruction on how to develop software programs and change careers to perform software development work. Now that I have been enrolled in a degree program for a while, it is clearly the right path for me. Even when debugging programs and feeling stumped on an efficient solution, I still feel joy when I go through that process and come up with a program that works. I particularly like the notion of being a controller of content moreso than being a content consumer.

When I am not studying software development, I love traveling, playing tennis, roller skating, and spending time with family and friends. Recently, I traveled to Minnesota for the first time and visited Paisley Park and the Mall of America. I also dined at a resturant called Hazelwood and had a slice of the most moist, delicious chocolate cake I've ever tased. I also dined at Hell's Kitchen. I envision traveling to Minnesota again in the future and am looking forward to attending a tennis retreat in Florida later this year.


My Goals for this Course


I am looking forward to fortifying my undertanding of web design and ending the class feeling highly confident in developing and publishing websites in accordance with standards and best practice. While I study the subject, I do hope to gain additional practice by helping to create a web presence for friends and family who have a need for such service. I'm also hoping the experience will culminate in a rewarding job opportunity that includes, as just one aspect of the work, designing and developing websites that help organizations achieve their business goals.
